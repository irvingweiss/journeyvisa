How it works

Four stages. You keep control of your own application the whole way through.

  1. Answer the questions

    You work through the application in short sections. Your answers save as you finish each one, and a reference number lets you come back at any time.

  2. We review what you entered

    Our team reads it properly. Anything incomplete, inconsistent or likely to raise a question comes back to you with a plain explanation of why.

  3. You confirm before anything is filed

    Nothing is submitted without your explicit sign-off. The application is signed under penalty of perjury, so the answers have to be yours.

  4. You go to the interview prepared

    We send the document list for your situation and what to expect on the day. The interview and the decision are between you and the consulate.

Questions people ask before they pay

Including the ones with answers you might not want to hear.

Are you part of the U.S. government?

No. Visa Journey USA is a private company with no affiliation to the Department of State or any other agency. You can apply on your own at travel.state.gov and pay only the government fee. We are an optional service for people who would rather have help.

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No, and nobody can. The decision rests entirely with a consular officer. What we can do is make sure your application is complete, accurate and consistent, which removes the avoidable reasons an application gets delayed or sent back.

Can you get me a faster interview appointment?

No. Appointment availability is set by the consulate, and we have no ability to move it. Anyone claiming otherwise is selling something they do not have.

What exactly am I paying you for?

Preparation and review. Our fee covers the guided application, the review of your answers and documents, and support by email through the process. It does not include the government fee, which you pay directly to the government.

What happens to my passport details?

They are used to prepare your application and nothing else. Nothing is stored in your browser, so a shared or stolen device does not expose them. You can ask us to delete your information at any point.

What if I decide I do not need the service?

Contact us. If you have not started, there is nothing to cancel. If you have, our refund terms are set out in the terms and conditions, and we will talk it through with you.

Who we are

Visa Journey USA is an independent preparation service based in the United States. We work with visitors from around the world who are planning a trip to the U.S. for tourism, to see family, or for temporary business.

We have no affiliation with the U.S. Department of State or any other government agency, and we do not represent ourselves as one. You can always apply directly at travel.state.gov and pay only the official fee. What we offer is a clearer, guided way to get through the same application, for people who would rather not do it alone.

We would rather tell you the service is wrong for you than take a fee you did not need to pay. If you do not need a visa, or you need a category we do not handle, we will say so.

What we do

The visa application asks for a great deal of detail, and small errors or omissions cause delays that were entirely avoidable. Our service covers:

  • Guidance through every part of the application, in plain language
  • A review of your answers and documents for completeness and accuracy
  • Explanations of the questions that most often confuse applicants
  • Preparation for your visa interview

We help you prepare and organise. The decision on any visa rests solely with the U.S. Department of State, and we never promise, expedite or guarantee a particular outcome. What we can promise is care, accuracy, and clear support through the process.
